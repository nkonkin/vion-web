## Design Context

### Users
Fans checking latest music and tour dates; bookers and press reaching out for bookings. They arrive from social links or search, often on mobile, usually at night or before a show.

### Brand Personality
Stark, architectural, nocturnal. Cool confidence — never hype clutter or festival-template energy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark-only, brutally minimal. References: Steve Angello, Axwell, John Summit — black void, high-contrast type, sparse typographic lists, logo as the hero signal. Anti: purple neon EDM templates, card grids, merch-store density, floating hero badges.

### Design Principles
1. Brand first — logo dominates the first viewport; no competing headline.
2. One job per section — Music, Tour, Contact only.
3. Lists over cards — releases and dates as typographic rows.
4. Motion is sparse — intentional entrance/hover only; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
5. No AI-default palette — near-black tinted cool gray, off-white type; no glow or purple accents.
