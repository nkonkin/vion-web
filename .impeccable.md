## Design Context

### Users
Fans checking latest music and tour dates; bookers and press reaching out for bookings. They arrive from social links or search, often on mobile, usually at night or before a show.

### Brand Personality
Stark, architectural, nocturnal. Cool confidence — never hype clutter or festival-template energy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark brutalist minimalism — single viewport, no page scroll, panel switching, raw 1px borders, tinted ink neutrals. Logo first in the content column. Open grotesques (General Sans labels + Switzer titles). Anti: blur, soft gradients, card grids, neon, condensed display, full-bleed splash heroes, all-caps body copy.

### Design Principles
1. Brand first — large logo anchors the left column; media fills the right.
2. One job per panel — Music, Tour, Contact; all releases kept.
3. Lists over cards — typographic rows; caps only for labels/actions.
4. Motion is sparse — cover crossfade only; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
5. Structure over style — visible rules, zero radius, no glow.
